# bestiary

Hostile creatures for the world outside the village. Fourteen species (tinted wolves, rams, hens and their elite or otherworldly cousins) spawn in a ring around every player who leaves the village, get tougher with distance from the centre of the overworld, and have their own rosters in each themed world. They wander near home, hunt players who come within range, give up and heal when dragged too far, and on death hand the killer experience, gold and loot — pelts, fangs, horns and the occasional draught or piece of gear — which the trader in town will buy. Monsters are ordinary NPCs driven by a `bestiary:hunter` behaviour registered through the npc feature's `npc:register-ais`; rewards go out through `character:grant`, and every kill is announced as `bestiary:slain` for quests to count. Monsters with no player within 80 tiles are culled, so the population stays bounded.
