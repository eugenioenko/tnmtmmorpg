# dungeons

Instanced caves. Entrances (🕳️, labelled with the cave's name and level) are scattered across the overworld as it is explored, with one guaranteed a short walk north of the village; caves get tougher the further they are from the village. Entering puts you in your own copy of the cave, and anyone who follows you in within thirty seconds shares it. A cave is a chain of chambers carved by core's `caves` generator, full of monsters the bestiary scales to the cave's level (`bestiary:spawn`), ending in a named guardian. Felling the guardian unseals its hoard, which each player in the run can open once for gold, experience, a piece of gear chosen by value from whatever `equipment` and `potion` items are installed, a trophy and cave crystals. The ladder by the entrance leads back out; falling in the cave sends you to the village as usual, and re-entering puts you back in the same copy. A copy that stands empty for a minute is cleared and reused.
