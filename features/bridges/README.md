# bridges

Build bridges across water, one plank at a time. A **Plank Walkway** (sawn from any two planks at the sawbench, three at a time, or bought from the trader) sits in your quick slots; stand at the water's edge and use it to turn the water tile ahead of you into a walkway, then step onto it and lay the next. The tile chosen is always the one ahead in the direction you last stepped, so walking out along your own bridge keeps extending it and nothing is laid sideways by surprise; to turn, step toward the water you want to build on. You get told when you are a few planks from the other side and when the bridge is complete. Walkways become core's walkable `bridge` tile, drawn as planks over the water, and they are permanent and shared: every bridge anyone builds stays for everyone. Caves and interiors have no open water to bridge.
