# party

Adventuring together. Invite another player with `/invite Name` in chat or from the Party window (`O`), which lists everyone nearby; they get a Join / Decline prompt. A party holds up to five. Members see each other's health and whereabouts in frames under their own (distance and a compass arrow, or which world they wandered into), a blue diamond floats over fellow members, and `/p text` talks on the party channel (`/p` alone lists the roster). When a member fells a monster, everyone else in the party within 30 tiles earns 60% of its experience and the kill counts toward their quests, so groups level and quest together. Party members always land in the same copy of a cave. The leader can `/kick`, `/promote` and invite; `/pleave` (or the Leave button) leaves. Parties are keyed by character name, so logging out keeps your place for five minutes; they are not persisted across a server restart. Experience goes out through `character:grant`; quest credit is a second `bestiary:slain` per helper marked `assist: true`; chat commands come through the chat feature's `chat:register-commands`. The public `party:tag { id }` component is what other features (and the markers) read to tell who is grouped with whom.
