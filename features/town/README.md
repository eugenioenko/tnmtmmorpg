# town

The village's people. Four named residents settle around the crossroads: Elder Aldous and Captain Brannoc hand out a chain of quests (slay, gather, visit another world, speak to someone), Morwen the Alchemist sells draughts and food, and Odo the Trader sells weapons, armor and charms and buys anything with a value — "sell all trophies" empties a pack of pelts and fangs in one click. Ordinary villagers can be chatted with for rumours. Conversations are a `dialog` component on the player whose options carry the intents they send, so the client renders any feature's dialog the same way. Quest progress lives in a `quests` component on the player; kills arrive from the bestiary's `bestiary:slain` event, and every reward or purchase goes through the character feature's `character:grant` / `character:charge`, so this feature never touches gold or experience itself. The client half adds the Journal window (J), an on-screen quest tracker, and "Talk to" for villagers.
