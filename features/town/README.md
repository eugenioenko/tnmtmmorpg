# town

The village's people. Four named residents settle around the crossroads: Elder Aldous and Captain Brannoc hand out a chain of quests (slay, gather, visit another world, speak to someone), Morwen the Alchemist sells draughts and food, and Odo the Trader sells weapons, armor and charms and buys anything with a value — "sell all trophies" empties a pack of pelts and fangs in one click. Ordinary villagers can be chatted with for rumours. Conversations are a `dialog` component on the player whose options carry the intents they send, so the client renders any feature's dialog the same way. Quest progress lives in a `quests` component on the player; kills arrive from the bestiary's `bestiary:slain` event, and every reward or purchase goes through the character feature's `character:grant` / `character:charge`, so this feature never touches gold or experience itself. The client half adds the Journal window (J), an on-screen quest tracker, and "Talk to" for villagers. The residents keep daily routines by the daynight feature's hour: the elder takes a morning stroll before sitting by the well, the captain eats at the inn at noon and walks the village bounds after dark, Morwen gathers by the lake at dusk, Odo drinks at the inn after closing, and everyone goes to bed (a drowsy *zZ* floats over them) at night. They still trade and talk wherever you catch them, with a line to match what they are doing, and they stand still while you talk to them. Without the daynight feature it is always noon.
