# critters

Ambient life drawn over the world, entirely on the client: butterflies over meadows by day, fireflies over grass, forest and swamp at night, leaves drifting down in the forest, fish rings rippling on water, and snow falling over the snowfields. Critters spawn on matching ground near you, using `client.tileAt`, `client.darkness` and `client.tileToScreen`, and stay out of interiors and caves. There is one `requestAnimationFrame` loop and no React work per frame.
