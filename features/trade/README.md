# trade

Player-to-player trading. Click another player ("Trade with") to ask; they get a prompt to accept or decline. Both then share a trade window: click items in your pack to put them up (right-click adds one at a time), click an offered item to take it back, and type an amount of gold. Each side locks its offer, then accepts; the swap happens in one step once both have accepted. Any change to either offer unlocks both sides, so nothing can be slipped in after the other player looked. Walking more than 8 tiles apart, dying or disconnecting cancels the trade; if a pack cannot hold what it is getting, or an offered item has left the pack, nothing changes hands. The whole exchange lives in each player's private `trade:state`, so bystanders never see the terms. Gold moves through the character feature's `character:charge` and `character:grant` events.
