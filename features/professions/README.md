# professions

Gathering trades that feed crafting. **Mining** works ore veins (copper, then iron at level 10, mithril at 20) that crop out beside hills; **woodcutting** fells the ordinary trees for pine and the rarer Elder Oaks and Ironwoods of the deep forest. Richer nodes appear further from the village, and a handful are placed within reach of it on first run. Click a node with a pickaxe or hatchet in your pack (the trader sells both) and you work it: a few seconds per swing, shown as a cast bar, repeating until the node is spent, your pack fills, you move or you are hit. Spent nodes recover after a while. Each skill levels from 1 to 50; higher levels swing faster and unlock richer nodes. The **Forge** and **Sawbench** in the village square (click either to open the crafting window) turn ore and logs into bars and planks, and those into swords, plate, totems and wards that equip through the character feature, which reads gear stats from `ItemDef.equip`. Skill levels persist per browser with their own token. Press `P` for the Professions window.
