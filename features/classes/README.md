# classes

Paths for players who reach level 5: **Warrior** (Fury), **Ranger** (Focus) or **Mystic** (Mana). Choosing one (press `L`; the window opens by itself the first time you are eligible) gives four abilities on `Q`, `F`, `G` and `T`, unlocked at levels 5, 6, 8 and 11 — Cleave, Shield Wall, Second Wind and Execute; Aimed Shot, Volley, Venom Arrow and Hunter's Mark; Firebolt, Mend, Frost Nova and Arcane Shield. Each costs from a pool that refills over time and has its own cooldown on top of a shared one-second global cooldown; some take a moment to cast (a cast bar shows it) and moving interrupts them, refunding the cost. Offensive abilities use your current target (click an enemy or press Tab). The feature never touches hit points itself: damage goes through combat's `combat:strike`, shields and marks through `combat:modifier`, and heals through character's `character:heal`. The pool, cooldowns and casts live in the private `classes:state`; the public `classes:class` names the path. The choice persists per browser with its own token; changing it later costs 50 gold.
